# Super Store Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a detailed view of sales and profitability across product categories, customer segments, regions, and time. The dataset is derived from a fictional retail superstore and is designed to support business insights and decision-making.

## 📊 Features
- Sales breakdown by Category and Sub-Category
- Profit and sales trends by Month and Year
- Regional and state-wise sales performance
- Segment analysis by Customer Type and Payment Mode
- Shipping Mode impact on sales
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) like Total Sales, Profit, Quantity, and Delivery Time

## 📈 KPIs Monitored
- **Total Sales**: $2M
- **Total Profit**: $175K
- **Quantity Sold**: 22K
- **Average Delivery Days**: 4

## 📦 Categories
- Office Supplies
- Technology
- Furniture

## 🗺 Regional Coverage
- Central, East, South, and West regions
- State-level granularity for top-performing states

## 🧩 Visualizations Used
- Bar Charts
- Line Charts
- Pie Charts
- KPI Cards
- Map Visualization

## 📥 How to Use
1. Open the `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop.
2. Interact with filters and slicers to explore various perspectives (year, segment, region).
3. Use the visual trends to drive business decisions.

## 📃 Notes
- The dataset is fictional and ideal for practice and educational purposes.
